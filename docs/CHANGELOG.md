# Changelog

Condensed from the project's measurement log and status notes. Dates are the dates the work was done. "Uploaded" means sent to the printer; "printed" means a part came off it; where a version was sliced but never sent, it says so.

| 2026-10-12 | **v11** | After printing the v10 test plate and the v2 extender plate, six changes: (1) **hook on the side walls only**, 9 mm each - the full-width bar fouled the board, which overhangs the end face by 3.6 mm; (2) pocket 1 mm deeper (`stack_h` 15) because the plate bowed upward when screwed down; (3) **extenders v3 = base + their own face plate**, so the top is textured and flush by construction; (4) a **second connector per seam**, a 21 x 12 x 3 mm top key under the plate, 17 mm above the spline; (5) smaller joint screws (clearance 3.2, counterbore 5.6 x 2.2, pilot 2.4); (6) caps v3, 5.2 mm tall and 12 mm wide (the undated v2 between had already turned the cap into a bell with a skirt). | 2026-10-21 | **v11 printed and assembled** | All three plates had come off the printer; the set was assembled with plate v9 on the cradle and put in use in front of the keyboard - the photograph in the [README](../README.md). No fit report has been written. |
| 2026-10-21 | repository | The parameter file gains `plate_style` (`palm` / `full`) and the pre-built STL matrix (`cad/variants.py` into `stl/`: three heights x hook / no hook, two face plates, joint keys, three cap fits); `cad/checks.py` as the geometric check suite; `cad/render.py` for the images in `docs/images/`. | 2026-10-22 | check-suite finding | `cad/checks.py` reports that the cradle's two front plate-screw pilots (x +-71.3, 3.2 mm from each seam) open into the spline seat after about 7.4 of their 10 mm, beside the spline's own M3 pilot. Moving the front screw row inboard of x +-65 would fix it but changes two holes in plate v9, which is printed - undecided. Not yet checked on the part. |

What has physically been printed, as of 2026-10-21: plate v1 (the 2026-09-24 fit), cradle v10, plate v9, the one-piece v2 extenders that reached the printer (the left one, and the outermost right one, which came off without its keyboard undercut - the extrude-reach bug), the v10 test plate, and the complete v11 set, assembled on 2026-10-21 and in use. Everything else in `stl/` is generated and unprinted.

## The mapper

| Date | Milestone | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-22 | **Phase 1 - hardware checklist passed** | Every input readable through the script's own functions on the bare board: 250 Hz with 0 gaps in 20 000 samples, sticks and triggers 0..255, touchpad two contacts + click, gyro / accelerometer, light bar 14 writes 0 failures, rumble bytes 0. L2/R2 found resting at 255 without the rear shell. Read + parse loop 5.3 % of one core. |
| 2026-09-22 | **Phase 2 - injection and feel** | Windows injection moves from `pynput` to `SendInput` through `ctypes` (relative motion, 1/120-notch wheel, scan codes). Touch motion tracked per contact id; touch sessions with tap detection; two-finger scroll; left-stick flicks with hysteresis; auto-repeat at the system rate; `tune.json` re-read live. Live-tested the same day; the default values stayed. Linux `evdev`/`uinput` backend written, untested on hardware. |
| 2026-10-05 | **Autostart (Windows)** | `ds4_autostart.pyw`: a hidden watcher that starts the mapper when the pad is present, restarts it when the pad returns, backs off after a quick crash, never doubles a hand-started mapper, and pauses on an `autostart.off` file. |
| 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-20 | **Pad dead: USB port board** | Around 2026-10-05 the pad stopped enumerating: no device, no error, no Kernel-PnP event on plug-in. Diagnosed as the micro-USB port board (JDS-055), the one part on its own ribbon; a new board and ribbon went in on 2026-10-20 (about $10, no soldering) and the watcher started the mapper by itself 2 s after it enumerated. See [HARDWARE.md](HARDWARE.md#the-usb-port-board-swap). |
| 2026-10-21 | **Control map v2** | Designed around the five buttons that physically exist (see [CONTROLS.md](CONTROLS.md)). Momentary **Fn** on L3 with Esc on a clean tap; tap-vs-hold on Share / Options with the modifier engaging at `hold_s`; the right stick as arrow keys with deflection-scaled repeat (`rstick_mode` keeps the old cursor behaviour); **key sequences** (`ctrl+k ctrl+left`); Fn + pad click to suspend; the light bar rewritten as a priority table with idle off, every colour steady; the attention file. Two bugs caught during the build: activity for the blue light could not come from raw stick values, and `last_input` was compared across two different clocks. Self-test green. |
| 2026-10-21 | **Repository** | The control map moves out of the code into `mapping.json` (loader, validator, `--map`, `--dump-map`; the in-code tables remain the defaults and round-trip). The Claude-specific state becomes the generic **attention** state (`attention_state.txt`, `--attention-file`), with Claude Code hooks as the documented example. `tools/mapping_editor.py` and `tools/render_map.py` are added; CI runs the self-tests on Ubuntu and Windows. |

Still open on the mapper: reconnect inside the mapper after an unplug (the watcher restarts it instead); re-releasing a key whose key-up Windows refused; tap-and-drag, palm rejection and per-app profiles; `cursor_centre` is a no-op on Linux (relative `uinput` device); over Bluetooth on Windows the padded `0x01` report is mistaken for USB, so full reports are never requested.
